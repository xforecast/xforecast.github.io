Multimodal, News-Grounded Financial Forecasting

Financial markets are a demanding stress test for machine learning: extreme non-stationarity, low signal-to-noise ratios, and adversarial dynamics. The XForecast Challenge, hosted at KDD 2026 Financial Day, invites participants to combine financial time-series with unstructured news text to forecast future stock prices under a realistic multimodal setting.

XForecast Challenge

A Kaggle competition on multimodal, news-grounded financial forecasting, organized as part of KDD 2026 Financial Day.

Join on Kaggle
Overview

The XForecast Challenge explores how modern AI models can combine financial time-series data with unstructured news text to improve stock forecasting. Participants are given historical stock prices together with associated financial news and are asked to predict future closing prices four weeks ahead.

The challenge focuses on 100 stocks by market capitalization and is designed to encourage research on multimodal learning, representation learning, and robust forecasting methods for financial markets.

Dataset

Historical stock prices paired with financial news, as well as the FinTexTS dataset for structured financial text and time-series alignment.

Modality

Multimodal forecasting with numerical signals and textual context.

Task

Predict stock closing prices 4 weeks ahead.

Evaluation

Ranked by a Hit Rate-based evaluation metric.
